# {{name}}: conventions for the AI assistant

{{base}}

This project started from the Endless Runner template: a three-lane runner on a road streamed from chunk prefabs. Components: `Runner`, `Track`, `TrackChunk`, `Socket`, `Obstacle`, `Coin`, `PowerUp`, `Sweeper`, `Debris`, `Backdrop`. Systems: `RunSystem` (spawns the `Runner` prefab at the `PlayerStart` empty, keeps the HUD's `ctx.store` values, opens `GameOver`), `RunnerSystem`, `TrackSystem` (spawns chunks ahead, destroys them behind), `CrashSystem`, `CoinSystem`, `PowerUpSystem`, `SweeperSystem`, `DebrisSystem`, `BackdropSystem`. Chunk prefabs are in the `Chunks` scene of `scenes/main.blend` and the other prefabs in its `Prefabs` scene; `src/lib/sockets.ts` fills a chunk's `Socket` empties from `ctx.random.stream('track')`. The run's coins are `ctx.store.coins`; distance and speed are `Runner` fields, copied into `ctx.store` for the HUD. The menus and HUD are Blender screens in `ui/ui.blend` (`Title`, `Pause`, `Options`, `GameOver`, `HUD`; `tools/build_ui.py` rebuilds it). The game opens on the title screen: playtests start with an Enter press. `tools/build_level.py` rebuilds the level and every prefab from scratch and discards edits made in Blender.

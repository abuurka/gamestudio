# Props from kits

Add game-ready props, change them, colour and place them

Click-by-click video tutorial, 1:45. GameStudio 0.1.0, Blender 5.2.2 or newer.

## 00:03 Step 1: Ask Claude Code for props

GameStudio's kits make game-ready props: rocks, crates, barrels, trees, fences and more. They're not in Blender's Add menu: you ask for them in Claude Code. Here: a crate, a barrel, a mossy rock and a pine tree.

## 00:18 Step 2: The props appear in Blender

Claude checks the kits and builds all four in your level. Each one is a finished model, standing on the lawn.

## 00:24 Step 3: Click the rock

To change one, select it first. Click the rock.

## 00:28 Step 4: Ask for a bigger rock

Then say what to change. Claude sees what you selected. Make this rock bigger, with a different shape.

## 00:34 Step 5: The rock rebuilds in place

It rebuilds the rock from its kit with a new size and a new seed. The seed picks the shape, and the same seed always gives the same rock.

## 00:44 Step 6: Click the barrel

Next, click the barrel.

## 00:46 Step 7: Ask for a palette colour

Now a colour. The kits share one material palette: stone, woods, metals, brick and more. Paint this barrel brick red.

## 00:54 Step 8: The barrel turns red

Claude gives the barrel's wood the palette's brick red. The iron hoops and the lid stay as they were.

## 01:00 Step 9: Open the Material tab

In the Properties, open the Material tab. There it is: G S Brick, the palette's shared brick material. Change it here, and every prop that uses it follows.

## 01:11 Step 10: Ask to stack it

Claude places props, too. Say: now put it on top of the crate.

## 01:16 Step 11: It snaps onto the crate

It moves the barrel over the crate and snaps it onto the first surface below, so nothing floats or sinks. The same snap sets props on hills and slopes.

## 01:26 Step 12: Turn on Game in the header

For a last look, turn on Game in the 3D View's header, to see the props the way the game draws them.

## 01:32 Done: Props from kits

That's it: ask for props from the kits, change their size, seed or colour, and place them. Every change Claude makes can be undone.
